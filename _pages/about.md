# Junteng Liu

I am a first-year PhD candidate in the HKUST NLP Group at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology. I am pursuing a Ph.D. in Computer Science (2024–Present) under the supervision of Professor Junxian He, who also previously advised me during my undergraduate studies at Shanghai Jiao Tong University.

I graduated from Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU) in June 2024 with a B.Eng. (2020–2024). My research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning.

## Research Interests and Skills

- Natural language processing
- Machine learning
- LLM reasoning and reinforcement learning
- Hallucination in vision-language models (VLM)
- LLM truthfulness and interpretability
